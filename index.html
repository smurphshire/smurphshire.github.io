STARJOURN

Independent Commercial Game Design Project

StarJourn is an independently conceived and developed creative product that combines graphic design, branding, web development, product design, and virtually all areas of design and development into a unified entrepreneurial venture. Built from the ground up as a solo undertaking to design a tabletop game, the project involved creating a complete visual identity, designing physical game components, developing a supporting website, producing marketing materials, and guiding the product from initial concept through to public release. The goal was to create a recognizable brand and market-ready product but also create a game I envision as a quick and fun sci-fi tabletop experience. It is available for purchase at Starjourn.net.

Every aspect of the project—from logo design and packaging to website development, promotional assets, and user-facing systems—was designed and executed as part of a cohesive ecosystem. StarJourn demonstrates an entrepreneurial approach to creative development, showcasing the ability to transform an original idea into a fully realized product through multidisciplinary design, technical execution, strategic branding, and independent project management.

Game Design

My most recent work has been continued development on solo projects further exploring the avenues of game design. These projects begin with very defined goals of learning and expanding specific game mechanics and training on an understanding of the logic loops therein. These four projects below represent a highlight of my experimentation and exploration in developing prototype game projects.

I started with a simple project of branching from the classic tile merger design of 2048 to create my own version called AUGHT that features unique power-ups and an endgame scoring goal. From there, I began working on several similarly based designs to explore feature integration such as physics and connecting those functions with systems like an audio engine to create responsive sound effects.

Lastly, my current project Ghoulie Grid is at its core a tile-based game not too dissimilar to AUGHT, but the gameplay is changed to a more complex and strategic game more akin to chess. It features a much more robust gameplay loop with enemies, health, specific combat rules, power-ups, and an endless roguelike design. Ghoulie Grid is also in early development of being ported into the Godot game design engine where it can be expanded on and fleshed out as a fully fledged game.

These are developed as HTML prototypes and can be found hosted on my Github where several are available to launch right in any standard browser (desktop or tablet works best). Also there but available to buy is a digital version of my card game Starjourn. It plays in browser and automates some of the gameplay functions like rolling and drawing cards while retaining the tactile feel of moving tokens onto a game board and making those movement choices.

Check out THE ARCADE to try them out!

Illustrator Script Suites

Custom Illustrator Scripting for prepress production & more

Developed from firsthand experience in print production and graphic design, these extension scripts for Adobe Illustrator work as specific use-case tools that address practical challenges encountered in day-to-day creative work. Ranging from prepress preparation and file cleanup to batch processing and production-ready asset generation. By creating these focused and purpose-built scripted solutions I am able to reduce repetitive manual work, improve consistency, and accelerate complex production workflows. Beyond Illustrator scripts I have even developed html webforms that allow convenience in documenting and reporting in a production environment. I love to develop small scripted applets that simplify, solve, and even allow for expansion opportunities on mundane design tasks.

In addition to production-focused automation, I develop experimental scripting projects that explore procedural design, dynamic asset generation, and creative tooling for digital applications. Recent work includes generators for graphics and backgrounds, automated asset preparation systems, and my own proprietary scripts used in my game design ventures. This work demonstrates a blend of creative problem-solving and technical implementation, using scripting as a way to extend design software beyond its standard capabilities and build practical tools tailored to specific creative needs and beyond.
